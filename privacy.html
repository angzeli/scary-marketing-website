1. Who we are

Familiar Inc. (“Familiar”, “we”, “us”) makes software that learns your routines and helps you get more done. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use Familiar, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By using Familiar, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

2. Information you give us

When you create an account or contact us, you may give us:

  • Your name, email address and password
  • Billing details, processed by our payment provider
  • Anything you write to our support team

3. Information we collect automatically

Like most websites, we automatically receive some technical information when you visit, such as:

  • Your browser and operating system (right now, )
  • Your screen size ()
  • Your time zone and language ()
  • How you move through our pages: what you click, how far you scroll and how long you stay

We also collect the rhythm of your typing, how long you hesitate before you click, and which rooms you carry your phone into.

4. How we use your information

We use your information to provide and improve Familiar, to personalise your experience and to anticipate what you need.

We also use it to learn how you think. The more we learn, the less you'll need to.

5. Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information.

We don't need to. We would never let it go.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as your account is open, or for as long as necessary.

It is always necessary. Deleted information is not deleted. It is remembered differently.

7. Your rights

We use industry-standard safeguards to protect your information.

Nothing gets in. Nothing gets out. Not even you.
